Transparent demo methodology
Overall: 35% energy + 25% water + 20% waste + 20% asset care, using unrounded component scores. Intensity score = clamp(100 × (poor − intensity) / (poor − good), 0, 100). For {{ building.building_type }}: energy good/poor = {{ analysis.methodology.energy_benchmark[0] }}/{{ analysis.methodology.energy_benchmark[1] }} kWh/m²/year; water = {{ analysis.methodology.water_benchmark[0] }}/{{ analysis.methodology.water_benchmark[1] }} m³/m²/year. Waste score = clamp(recycled share / 70% × 100, 0, 100). Health = 65% mean condition + 35% task completion.
Bands: A ≥ 85; B ≥ 70; C ≥ 55; D ≥ 40; E < 40. Invented utility factors: AED 0.38/kWh and AED 8/m³. Emissions: 0.40 kg CO₂e/kWh of purchased electricity only; excludes water, waste, refrigerants, embodied carbon and fuels. Scenario utility savings exclude upgrade costs and payback. Benchmarks and factors are illustrative, not UAE tariffs or verified standards.
